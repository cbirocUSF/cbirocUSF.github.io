THE RESET BEGINS / 2022

The easy-growth era hits a wall.

As capital costs rose and public software valuations reset, SaaS leaders put a sharper lens on spend. Sales teams faced more scrutiny, slower approvals, and a renewed need to connect each purchase to a business outcome.

02 / The current playbook

The deal still
runs on trust.

Tools change the pace. The durable edge is still doing the hard, human parts of selling unusually well.

01 — GET SPECIFIC

Sell the result, not the category.

Translate features into the buyer’s own operating metrics. A credible business case beats another broad promise of transformation.

02 — MAKE IT EASY

Respect the buying group.

Give champions useful material to share, answer finance and security concerns early, and make the next step obvious to every stakeholder.

03 — USE AI WELL

Automate prep. Keep judgment.

Use AI for research, synthesis, and admin. Verify the output, protect customer data, and keep discovery and negotiation genuinely human.
